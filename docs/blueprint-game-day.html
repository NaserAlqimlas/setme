SetMe game day

System overview

A game day turns a WhatsApp conversation into four balanced teams of six, a run of games to 25, and results players can see. The organizer drives it on a phone at the hall. Players take part through WhatsApp and, once their account is linked, through Sign up, Lineup and Me. Behind it sit a Supabase database with row-level security, two Netlify functions that read ratings players and organizers can't, and SQL upgrades the super runs by hand.

Out of scope: rating (Rate, rate-queue), invites and account creation, match-stat tagging, season recap.

Pain points

  • The playing list has two sources that overwrite each other.
  • The playing list carries over from last week.
  • The game date is entered three times and checked once.
  • Unlinked members can only sign up through WhatsApp.
  • Players see nothing until Show to players is turned on.
  • Every step needs a connection in the hall.

Process architecture

Decision pointBranchesWho decides
Where does the playing list come from?Sign-ups (Set as Playing list) or the WhatsApp paste. Whichever runs last wins. See "Two sources overwrite the playing list".Organizer
Full or not?Under the cap: in. At the cap: waitlist in sign-up order. A drop-out moves the first waitlisted player in.The database, by order
Which option to save?One of the built options, after swaps; or teams the organizer pasted; or a saved draftOrganizer
A date already has teamsReplace after a confirm; saved games keep the players they hadOrganizer
A game ran out of timeSaved as it stood; scaled to a game to 25 in InsightsOrganizer, by entering the score
Is the day over?End the day, or leave it open. See "Day never ended".Organizer

The sequence that must hold: the playing list before Build teams; the Teams date before Save for scores; Save for scores before any game; Show to players before players can see anything. Sign-ups and the WhatsApp list run in parallel all week. Everything from Build teams on happens in the hall before the first game. Every step is a single live write by one organizer, so the process holds at this group's size; the manual joins (date, publish, end) are where it slips, not volume.
