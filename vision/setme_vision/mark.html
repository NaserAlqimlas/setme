Court marks

Click each point of the volleyball court where its lines meet (the outside edge of the lines), in the order on the right. The centre line is the one straight under the net, through the feet of the net posts; the attack lines are 3 m either side of it. If a point is out of shot or hidden, press Can't see it. After four, the whole court is drawn back in blue: if the blue lines sit on the real lines everywhere, save. Pick a moment with few people on the lines. S can't see it · ⌫ undo · Enter save.
